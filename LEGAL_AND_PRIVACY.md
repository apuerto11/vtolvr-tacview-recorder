## Distribution boundary

Packages contain this mod's original DLL and installation metadata only.
They contain no game assemblies, Tacview models, extracted maps, textures,
generated terrain caches, recordings, private logs, credentials, or PDB files.
Terrain heightfields are generated locally from each user's installed game.
Do not redistribute those derived caches or extracted game assets.

## Privacy

The recorder has no automatic telemetry, crash-reporting, or upload channel.
Recordings can contain pilot names, mission information and local paths.
Before manually sharing a bug report, follow [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) and
redact private information. Never attach terrain RAW/XML files or credentials.

## Publication decision

remaining licensing uncertainty, without claiming individual written permission.
If the rights holder objects, the owner intends to take the mod offline.
This records the owner's decision; it is not a legal opinion or a permission grant.
